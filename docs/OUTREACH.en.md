## Short announcement

I am building Lecture PPT Workflow for instructors and teaching assistants who already have lecture notes and a reference PowerPoint deck.

The workflow helps an agent organize teaching slides, preserve editable content and speaker notes, and revise a chapter from instructor feedback. It includes an original before/after example and local checks for content coverage and layout.

The early release requires a local Codex environment and external Python/Node/rendering dependencies. The bundled demo is in Chinese; English setup instructions and prompts are included, while English-course output still needs independent validation.

Try the small bundled example first, then tell me where you got stuck or whether you created your own slides:

- [Source and demo](https://github.com/hhhmike240-maker/lecture-ppt-workflow)
- [Feedback](https://github.com/hhhmike240-maker/lecture-ppt-workflow/issues/new/choose)

## Instructor group invitation

Do you prepare new lecture chapters from notes and an existing slide template? I am looking for a few instructors or TAs to try an early teaching-slide workflow on a small, non-sensitive example. I would value feedback on setup, missing knowledge points, speaker notes, and the revision process. You do not need to share private course materials or student information.

Start with the [sample deck](https://github.com/hhhmike240-maker/lecture-ppt-workflow/tree/main/demo). If you try it, please [report your result](https://github.com/hhhmike240-maker/lecture-ppt-workflow/issues/new/choose), even if installation failed.

## Where to start

1. Publish the bilingual documentation and working feedback entry point on GitHub.
2. Share the small example with an instructor/TA group where you are already a participant and project posts are allowed. Lead with the teaching problem and ask for a concrete trial outcome.
3. Share a technical walkthrough with an agent-tooling or open-source group whose rules permit it, emphasizing reproducibility and current limits.
4. Consider Show HN only when strangers can actually try the project and you can answer questions. Its [official rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) ask for something runnable and discourage barriers, promotional vote requests, and minor-update submissions. A translated README alone is not a sufficient launch story.
