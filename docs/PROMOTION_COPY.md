## 中文发布帖

我开源了一个面向教师和助教的 Codex Skill：`lecture-ppt-workflow`。

它不是“Word 自动转 PPT”，而是一套有人审核的教学课件工作流：输入讲义、自己的标准 PPT 和本次制作范围，按课程知识组织页面，保留授课备注和可编辑内容，再根据教师反馈迭代后续章节。

项目还提供 Word/PPT 索引、知识覆盖检查、样式检查和最小演示示例。

适合：

- 有讲义和标准课件的高校教师、助教
- 需要复用课程版式和授课备注的教研团队
- 希望把密集内容改造成情境任务和点击揭示分析的教学设计者

当前是早期试用版，需要 Node、Python、中文字体和演示软件；复杂模板、跨平台渲染和动态放映仍需使用者自行验证。欢迎先复现短讲义示例，再提交脱敏反馈。

[查看源码和原创示例](https://github.com/hhhmike240-maker/lecture-ppt-workflow) · [报告试用结果](https://github.com/hhhmike240-maker/lecture-ppt-workflow/issues/new/choose)

## 英文发布帖

I released `lecture-ppt-workflow`, a Codex Skill for instructors and teaching assistants.

It is not a Word-to-PowerPoint converter. It is a human-reviewed workflow that combines lecture notes, a reference deck, and instructor feedback to produce reusable teaching slides while preserving course knowledge, layout conventions, speaker notes, and editable content.

The early preview also includes Word/PPT indexing, content-coverage checks, style checks, and a small reproducible demo. It currently requires Python, Node, fonts, and presentation software. Complex templates, cross-platform rendering, and dynamic playback still need user validation.

The bundled example is Chinese; English setup instructions and prompts are included in 0.1.1-alpha. English-course output has not yet been independently validated. [Try the example](https://github.com/hhhmike240-maker/lecture-ppt-workflow) and [share your result](https://github.com/hhhmike240-maker/lecture-ppt-workflow/issues/new/choose).
